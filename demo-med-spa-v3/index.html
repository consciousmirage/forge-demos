Corrective Facials

Dermaplaning

Good to know

Before your first visit.

I’ve never had a professional facial. Where do I start?

With a corrective facial. Tasneem analyzes your skin and concerns first, then builds a plan around your goals — it’s the best way to understand your skin and where it can go.

What treatments do you offer?

Corrective facials, dermaplaning, microdermabrasion, enzyme treatments, BioRePeel and chemical peels, brow lamination with hybrid tint, and lash lifts — plus medical-grade home-care to protect your results.

Is this a private setting?

Yes. your skin is a private, woman-owned suite where you’re seen one-on-one. It’s a women-only clinic, designed to feel calm and unhurried from the moment you arrive.

Do at-home products really matter?

They do — what you use daily makes up the majority of the results you see. Tasneem recommends only medical-grade products, chosen specifically for your skin, never an upsell.
