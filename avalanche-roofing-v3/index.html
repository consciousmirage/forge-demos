What we do

Every roof on the Wasatch Front, residential to commercial.

One in-house crew — no subcontractors, no piece-rate. From a full tear-off to a leak repair, the same team that bids your roof is the team that finishes it.

Roof Replacement

Full tear-off and a new GAF shingle system — qualifying installs are eligible for the GAF System Plus limited warranty. Many re-roofs are done in a single day.

Roof Repair

Leaks, storm and wind damage, and missing or worn shingles — fixed fast so a small problem stays a small problem.

Flat & Commercial Roofing

TPO membrane, EPDM, PVC, IB, and metal roofing systems for flat and low-slope commercial buildings across the valley.

Seamless Rain Gutters

New seamless gutter installation and gutter clean-out to move water off the roof and away from your foundation.

Swamp Cooler Removal

Removing an old evaporative cooler before a re-roof — a common Utah job we handle as part of the project, sealed clean.

Job-Site Clean-Up

Thorough clean-up after every job — the part customers mention most. Nails magnet-swept, debris hauled, yard left how we found it.

Straight answers

What homeowners ask first.

What does an estimate cost?

Nothing. Estimates are free, and customers report a fair, competitive bid back within about 24 hours of the visit.

How long does a full re-roof take?

Many residential re-roofs — tear-off included — are finished in a single day. We’ll give you a realistic timeline with your bid.

Are you a GAF-certified roofer?

Yes. Avalanche is a GAF Certified™ contractor, which means qualifying roof systems are eligible for the GAF System Plus limited warranty.

Do you do flat and commercial roofs?

Yes — TPO membrane, EPDM, PVC, IB, and metal systems for flat and low-slope commercial buildings, alongside our residential shingle work.

Who actually does the work?

The Martinez family crew. Every employee is hourly — no subcontractors, no piece-rate — so the team that bids your roof is the team that builds it.
