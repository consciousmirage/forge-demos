Since 1983

A West Valley original, run by the same family.

George and Karee Marinos opened Ogie's at the corner of 3500 South and Redwood Road in 1983 — “a mom-and-pop place, a greasy spoon, just breakfast and lunch.” Forty-one years later it's still exactly that, and still in the family.

The name comes from a nickname George's older brothers gave him on a 1963 family trip to Yellowstone. Today his daughter Cami runs the floor, with cooks and servers who've been here fifteen to twenty years. As George puts it, “I think it's just a friendly place.”
