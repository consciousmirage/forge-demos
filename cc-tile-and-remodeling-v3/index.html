Questions

Before you call.

Can I actually see the tile before I commit?

Yes — that's the point of the showroom. Visit 3410 S 300 W and see finished tile applications in person: floors, showers, countertops, wall tile, and backsplashes. Call first and we'll set a time.

Do you help with design, or just installation?

Both. CC Tile has its own in-house designer who helps you work out the style, type of tile, and layout for your home or business before installation starts.

Do you handle full remodels or only tile?

Full remodels too. Our public permit record includes whole-interior remodels up to $158,000 — kitchens, basements, bathrooms, and commercial spaces. We can act as your general contractor.

What areas do you serve?

Salt Lake City, Murray, Millcreek, and South Salt Lake — the showroom is at 3410 S 300 W, and most of our work is within a short drive of it.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (801) 463-9100. Estimates are free, and if you can visit the showroom first, even better — seeing the tile in person beats any photo.
