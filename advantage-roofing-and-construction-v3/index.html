Straight answers

What homeowners ask first.

What does an inspection or estimate cost?

Nothing. Inspections and estimates are free. You get an honest read on what needs fixing now and what can safely wait — no pressure either way.

How long does a full re-roof take?

Once the crew is on site, most residential re-roofs — tear-off included — finish in about two days.

What warranty do I get?

A 10-year workmanship guarantee on most work, on top of the manufacturer’s warranty on the roofing system itself (Owens Corning, CertainTeed, or Tamko).

Will you work with my insurance company?

Yes. For wind, storm, and hail claims we document the damage and deal directly with your insurer so the claim reflects what your roof actually needs.

Do you only do roofs?

No — Advantage is a licensed general contractor with a carpentry specialty. Decks, porches, pergolas, gutters, and sheet metal are all built by the same in-house crew.
