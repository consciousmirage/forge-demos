Good to know

Questions new patients ask first.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes — new patients of every age are welcome, from a toddler’s first visit to seniors needing dentures. Call (801) 355-5558 and we’ll get you scheduled.

Do you take my insurance?

We file with most major PPO dental plans and submit the claim for you. Bring your card to your first visit and we’ll verify your benefits and explain any out-of-pocket cost before we begin.

I’m anxious about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Patients consistently mention how comfortable and unhurried our team makes them feel, and we offer sedation options for those who need them. Tell us at booking and we’ll plan around it.

Do you do implants and bigger work, or just cleanings?

Both. Alongside routine care, Dr. Matt has advanced training in implants, sedation and full-mouth reconstruction — so most treatment stays in this office instead of being referred across town.

How long has the practice been here?

Since 1954, in the same downtown Salt Lake City location — now in its third generation of Larson dentists. Many families have trusted us for decades.
