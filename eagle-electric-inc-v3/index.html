Since 1987

Founded by one electrician. Bought by the crew who built it.

Kurt Brooks started Eagle Electric in 1987 on three things: integrity, service, and craftsmanship. In 2011, the key employees who had spent their careers here bought the company from him — and kept running it the same way.

Today it's led by Trent Lovendahl and Doug Hawkes, with Jodie Turner running the office — the same Jodie customers call out by name in reviews. Most of the core crew has been here more than a decade.
