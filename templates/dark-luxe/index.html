The Room

Low light, long tables,
and a hearth that
never goes cold.

We built The Ember Room around a single open fire. Brass, leather, and oiled walnut catch the glow; the rest is left dark on purpose. It is the kind of room you sink into for three hours and forget the city outside.

Every cut is dry-aged in-house for a minimum of 35 days and finished over white-oak embers by a kitchen that has cooked here, together, since the night we opened.
