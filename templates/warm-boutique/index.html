Our roastery

Started by two neighbors who just wanted a better cup.

Marigold & Oat opened in 2016 in a tiny corner shop with one secondhand roaster and a borrowed mixer. We didn't know much except that the coffee in our neighborhood could be a lot better — so we learned, batch by batch.

Eight years later we're still on the same corner, still roasting by hand, and still tasting every batch before it hits the shelf. The only thing that's grown is the line out the door on Saturday mornings.
