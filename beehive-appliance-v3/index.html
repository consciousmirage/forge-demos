What we fix

Every major appliance in your home.

From dishwashers to dryers, we repair it all — expert service at a reasonable price. If it runs in your kitchen or laundry room, chances are we’ve fixed one before.

Washers

Won’t spin, won’t drain, leaking or off-balance — we diagnose and fix front- and top-loaders fast.

Dryers

No heat, no tumble, loud or taking forever to dry — gas and electric dryers serviced and repaired.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Not cooling, ice maker out, frost build-up or strange noises — keep your food cold and your fridge running.

Ovens, Ranges & Cooktops

Burners not heating, uneven baking or a dead control board — gas and electric stoves, ovens and cooktops.

Dishwashers

Not draining, not cleaning, leaking onto the floor — we pinpoint the issue, often in minutes.

Microwaves & More

Built-in microwaves, trash compactors and garbage disposals too. Don’t see it listed? We probably still repair it.

Straight answers

What folks ask first.

What appliances do you repair?

Washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, dishwashers, ranges, stoves, ovens, cooktops, microwaves, trash compactors and disposals. Don’t see yours listed? We probably still repair it — just give us a call.

What brands do you service?

All major makes and models — Whirlpool, GE, Maytag, Kenmore, KitchenAid, LG, Frigidaire, Electrolux, Sub-Zero, Viking, Wolf, Thermador and many more.

What areas do you cover?

The Salt Lake Valley, Southern Davis County and Northern Utah County — including Salt Lake City, Murray, Sandy, Draper, West Jordan, South Jordan, Riverton and Taylorsville.

Will you tell me if it’s not worth fixing?

Yes. Customers thank us for this in review after review — if repairing your appliance would cost about as much as replacing it, we’ll be straight with you and say so.

How do I get started?

Give us a buzz at (801) 523-8184. Dean answers, talks through what’s wrong, and gets you scheduled — and we’ll “bee” right there.
