What we fix

Every major appliance in your home.

From the fridge to the laundry room, we repair it all — with genuine factory parts and a one-year warranty on every job. If it runs in your kitchen or laundry room, we have fixed one before.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Not cooling, ice maker out, frost build-up or strange noises — keep your food cold and your fridge running.
