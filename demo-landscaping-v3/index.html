Straight answers

What homeowners ask first.

How do I get started?

Every project starts with a consultation — an on-site walk with you to understand your vision, needs, and budget. You’ll get a detailed bid before we break ground, so let’s talk through your ideas first.

Do you do both design/build and maintenance?

Yes. We design and install full custom landscapes, and we maintain them — whether we built your yard or you just need help keeping what you’ve got looking its best year-round.

What does a full landscape include?

Water features, patios & walkways, outdoor lighting, structures like pergolas and gazebos, sprinkler installs, sod, new installations, and refreshes — customized to your taste and your lot.

Are your designs eco-conscious?

We put sustainability into practice on every project: green waste gets composted and returned to the soil, concrete is crushed and reused, and we prioritize low-water, Utah-friendly plants. Nothing hits the landfill unless it absolutely has to.

What maintenance services do you offer?

Weekly mowing, trimming & edging, sprinkler repairs, spring & fall clean-ups, hauling and debris removal, lawn aerating & power raking, soil & mulch installation, and tree trimming and hedge pruning.
