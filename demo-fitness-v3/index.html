Programs

Training for the whole family, beginner to fighter.

Every program is built around practical self-defense — techniques for real situations, not points. Start with a free intro class and find the right fit.

Adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Gi and No-Gi grappling under a Team Pedro Sauer lineage. Build control, leverage, and composure from your first roll.

Muay Thai & Thai Boxing

The art of eight limbs — fists, elbows, knees, and shins — taught under World Thai Boxing Association certification.

Kali / Filipino Martial Arts

Stick, blade, and empty-hand weapon defense and disarming — the Filipino fighting systems, taught from day one.

Jeet Kune Do

The Founder

Will Bernales has taught some students since they were four.

Chief instructor Will Bernales built BIMA around one idea: martial arts should be a "seatbelt for life" — knowledge and skill that protect you whether you ever need them or not. He holds rank and teaching certification across multiple systems, and he's known for the patience and mentorship that keep families training for years, not months.

  • Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — 4th-degree black belt, Team Pedro Sauer lineage
  • Kali / FMA — full instructor under Guro Dan Inosanto's curriculum
  • Muay Thai — certified, World Thai Boxing Association
  • Combat Submission Wrestling — Utah state representative
  • Jeet Kune Do Concepts — certified instructor
